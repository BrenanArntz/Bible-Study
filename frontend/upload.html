Upload handwritten notes

Take a photo of a page or choose an image from your phone. The original image is saved with its Markdown note.

Select pages in reading order. Auto-categorization uses the transcription; an E-number such as E001 creates an exegesis note, and following continuation pages in this batch join that note. You can also append pages to an existing note.

For new notes titled with a passage such as Acts 10-11, verse-numbered lines are saved as full Bible references. Add chapter headings such as “Acts 10” and “Acts 11” so each verse is assigned to the right chapter.

Images are stored privately in Supabase. Gemini transcription runs through a Supabase Edge Function; browser OCR is used if Gemini is not configured.
